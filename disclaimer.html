Escalation, deadlines and filing

Complaint, internal review, tribunal review, appeal, judicial review and enforcement are different processes. Eligibility, jurisdiction, prior steps, notices, certificates, referrals, time limits and exceptions depend on the particular route. Read the linked official requirement and your actual decision or notice.

Starting a case in ClarityPath, contacting a help service or making a general complaint does not itself lodge a legal application or automatically stop another filing deadline. The application does not automatically contact institutions, serve documents or retain a representative. Urgent and safety-related matters may require a direct or exceptional route rather than ordinary complaint stages.

Official starting points: NCAT application requirements and Legal Aid NSW / LawAccess NSW. Assistance and representation depend on the service’s scope and eligibility.

Evidence, summaries and AI

Your account, original documents, interpretation, review and advocacy are kept as distinct kinds of material. Recording an account or applying a software qualification does not determine its admissibility or the weight a court or other decision-maker will give it. Firsthand evidence can be recorded without an extra attachment.

Summaries and submission drafts can omit context or contain mistakes. Optional AI output can misstate facts or law and supply incorrect citations. Check the actual linked source, quotation, date and applicability before using a draft. A hash helps check whether bytes changed; it does not prove that the original account or document is true. No outcome, funding, representation or institutional response is guaranteed.

Private records and external services

The public site and public source repository are separate from private case storage. Opening the site does not automatically publish your private case. Temporary in-memory work can be lost unless you save or download it. Encrypted device storage, private GitHub storage and optional AI assistance require their respective setup; read the operating guide and current implementation status.

Keep passwords, repository tokens, encryption keys and identifiable case material out of public repositories and public issue reports. Review selected material before exporting a readable handoff or approving an external AI request. A recipient can read an unencrypted handoff. Hosting, GitHub, external source websites and any configured AI provider have their own data-handling practices. This site does not claim an independent security audit or absolute confidentiality.
